💡 Key Business Insights & Strategic Action Items

Critical Risk

Product Overload Anomaly (3+ Products)

Customers holding 3 or 4 bank products experience an alarming 78%+ churn rate. Halt aggressive cross-selling past 2 products and focus on UX adoption.

Demographic Insight

Senior Segment Attrition (>50 Years)

Chi-square hypothesis test confirms senior customers churn at double the base rate (34.2% vs 15.1%). Deploy specialized senior wealth advisory managers.

Retention ROI

Engagement Activation Playbook

Inactive members account for 61% of total churn volume. Targeted fee-waiver reactivation campaigns will recover up to ₹1.2Cr in annual deposits.
